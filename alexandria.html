    Engine Architecture

    Local inference. Full control. The studio writes the spec; the Alexandria bridge on your computer hands it to an open model and returns the audio. Your brief, your prompt and your audio stay on your machine.

    YuE

    Two-stage language model over audio codec tokens: a 7B stage-1 model writes the song's tokens section by section, a 1B stage-2 model fills in acoustic detail. It reads short space-separated tags (genre, instrument, mood, gender, timbre), not long prose, and about 30 seconds of audio per lyric section.

    ACE-Step

    Diffusion model. Version 1.5 pairs an optional language-model planner with a diffusion transformer, has Windows launch scripts, and runs on small GPUs (its smallest setup targets 6 GB or less). It takes the comma-separated style prompt directly, plus tagged lyrics, BPM, key and duration.

    House Rules
    
          
    
            
    Style prompt
    2,000 characters maximum, counted by the app on every spec. Vocal profiles and beat-switch descriptors are protected; trims follow dedupe → connectives → merged overlaps → weakest descriptor.
    
            
    Runtime
    3:05 – 3:35, computed from bar counts at tempo (4/4).
    
            
    Tag canon
    Cold open (no [Intro] opener). Always [Chorus], never [Hook]. The app corrects both automatically.
    
            
    Package
    Every spec carries a style prompt, lyrics, Weirdness, Style Influence and a Beat Blueprint.
